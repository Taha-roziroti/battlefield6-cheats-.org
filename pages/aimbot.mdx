# Battlefield 6 Aimbot: Features and Aim Controls

The aim menu groups activation, view area, and targeting so you can build a layout without flipping every toggle at once.

## Set an activation style that suits your setup

Toggle or always-on, plus a custom aim bind. Choose how the feature turns on before you touch FOV or smoothness.

## Adjust smoothness, field of view, and distance

Smoothness, FOV, and distance are all adjustable. Change one setting, check the result, then move to the next.

## Choose the target and bone options

Head, neck, body, or pelvis; bot or team filters; visibility check; optional FOV border and background markers for clarity in-menu.

## Keep configurations organized

Save, load, share, and delete named configs in the configuration manager. Menu and unload bindings sit alongside other interface controls.

## Windows requirements

Windows 10 or 11 on Intel or AMD with HVCI, Core Isolation, TPM, and Secure Boot enabled. Details: [Battlefield 6 cheat requirements](/battlefield-6-cheat-requirements/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which aim settings can be customized?

Activation style, aim bind, smoothness, FOV, distance, bones, bot/team targeting, visibility checks, and FOV displays.

### Can the aim configuration be saved?

Yes—the configuration manager supports save, load, share, and delete.

### What operating systems are listed?

Windows 10 and 11 with the security settings listed in the requirements guide.
